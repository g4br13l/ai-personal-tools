To perform a thorough due diligence on foreign tech companies, you should cross-reference data from three main pillars: financial health, real compensation, and internal organizational culture. The ideal strategy is to validate a recruiter's promises using legal public records and anonymous testimonials from verified employees.

Here are the best sources to collect this information:

### 1. Financial Health and Investment Rounds

- **[Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com):** The primary source for private companies. It allows you to view investment rounds, lead investors, and IPO or acquisition predictions.
- **([https://finance.yahoo.com](https://finance.yahoo.com)):** Essential for public companies listed on the stock exchange, providing balance sheets and earnings reports.
- **[FundedIQ](https://fundediq.co):** Provides curated intelligence on startups that have recently received funding, which serves as a primary indicator of hiring capacity.

### 2. Salaries (Minimum, Maximum, and Average)

- **[Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi):** The best tool for understanding "Total Compensation" (TC), including base salary, bonuses, and equity (RSUs/Options). It also maps seniority levels across different companies.
- **([https://h1bdata.info](https://h1bdata.info)):** Contains the actual base salaries that U.S. companies legally declared to the government when hiring foreign workers.
- **[Comprehensive.io](https://www.comprehensive.io):** Aggregates salary ranges from over 6,000 tech companies, updated daily in accordance with U.S. pay transparency laws.
- **([https://wellfound.com](https://wellfound.com)):** Focused on startups, this platform openly displays salaries and the percentage of equity offered for various roles.

### 3. Satisfaction and Organizational Culture

- **([https://www.teamblind.com](https://www.teamblind.com)):** An anonymous forum that requires a work email for verification. This is where employees discuss the unfiltered reality of bonuses, management, layoffs, and culture.
- **([https://www.inhersight.com](https://www.inhersight.com)):** Designed for women’s experiences, it rates companies on female leadership, equal opportunities, and family support.
- **[FairyGodboss](https://fairygodboss.com):** A community for women offering reviews on diversity, inclusion, and specific benefits.
- **[Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com):** Useful for general ratings, CEO approval, and specifically for reading detailed accounts of interview questions.

### 4. Headcount and Structure

- **[LinkedIn](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.linkedin.com):** Allows you to see the total number of employees, growth by department, and average employee tenure.
- **([https://builtin.com](https://builtin.com)):** Provides detailed profiles of tech companies, including team size and the specific benefits infrastructure they offer.
